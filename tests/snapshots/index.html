Keep in mind
  • ctrl has only 2 sample(s). Tests between conditions use the sample as the replicate, so with this few sections only large, consistent differences can be detected.
  • Every 'donor' value occurs in one condition level only, so condition and donor are confounded: a difference between conditions can be a difference between donors.
  • Visium spots (55 µm) each hold several cells (typically 1–10): expression, domains and spatially variable genes describe spot mixtures, not single cells.

Spatial domains

  • BANKSY found 4 domains (λ = 0.8, resolution 0.2, harmony integration); 3 are present (≥ 20 spots) in at least two samples, 3 in all 4.
  • Confined to a single sample: D04.
  • D01 covers 43% of all spots (markers SFTPB, SFTPC, NAPSA).
  • D02 covers 33% of all spots (markers IGHG1, IGKC, JCHAIN).
  • D03 covers 22% of all spots (markers COL1A1, DCN, LUM).
  • Present in one condition only: D04 (treat: s3).
  • Agreement with the user annotation: ARI 0.35, NMI 0.42 pooled; per sample ARI 0.26 (s3) to 0.58 (s2), over 1,200 annotated spots (Unassigned left out).
  • Best-matching domains: TLS → D02 (precision 0.25, recall 0.75).
  • No domain matches Tumor well (best F1 below 0.3).
Keep in mind
  • ARI rewards getting the large classes right, not small structures; a modest ARI is expected when the annotation has a few coarse classes and many unannotated spots.
  • Domains are clusters, not anatomy: BANKSY makes clusters spatially smooth but does not know what a structure is. One region can split over several domains and a domain can mix tissues that share a neighbourhood profile; look at the maps and markers before naming them.
  • Harmony removed differences between samples before clustering, including real ones: a region that exists in only some sections, or that a condition changes, can be pulled into another domain.
  • With λ = 0.8 the neighbourhood dominates: structures only a few spots across are smoothed into their surroundings, and boundaries between regions can form thin domains of their own.

Spatially variable genes

  • Spatially variable genes (Moran's I, FDR 0.05) per sample: 13 (s4) to 19 (s3) of the 64 genes tested in each.
  • 41 of 64 genes tested are significant in at least one sample, 3 in all 4.
  • Most consistently spatially variable: COL1A1 (4/4, mean I 0.45), IGHG1 (4/4, mean I 0.43), SFTPB (4/4, mean I 0.43), G036 (3/4, mean I 0.03), G049 (3/4, mean I 0.02), G028 (2/4, mean I 0.03), G027 (2/4, mean I 0.03) and G047 (2/4, mean I 0.03) (significant in / tested in samples).
  • 4 of the top 20 are also top-10 markers of a domain or cluster, e.g. COL1A1 (D03), IGHG1 (D02), SFTPB (D01) and KRT5 (D04): their spatial pattern largely follows where that region or cell type lies.
  • 79% of the significant gene × sample results have I < 0.05: statistically detectable, but a weak pattern.
Keep in mind
  • Significance is not effect size: with thousands of spots the analytic test calls genes with I of 0.02–0.03 significant. Read I and the rank, not only the count of significant genes.
  • In spot data a gene is spatially variable mostly because the cells expressing it are arranged in space (immunoglobulins follow plasma cells); this does not separate composition from regulation within a cell type.
  • Moran's I depends on the neighbour graph and density: compare values between samples only when they were built the same way. It measures one global pattern; a gene patterned in a small region of a large section scores low.

domain: treat vs ctrl (condition; ctrl 2 and treat 2 samples)

  • Composition (treat vs ctrl): 1 label of 4 differ at FDR 0.05: D02 30% → 45% (adj. p 0.004).
  • Pseudobulk DE (treat vs ctrl): 12 significant gene × label results at FDR 0.05 over 3 tested labels; most in D01 (4: 3 up, 1 down in treat).
  • Not tested (1 label): D04 (reference level 'ctrl' has fewer than 2 pseudobulks).
  • 3 genes change the same way in at least 3 of the 3 tested labels (up: IFI6, ISG15; down: CRYAB): a shift of the whole tissue between conditions rather than of one region or cell type.
Keep in mind
  • The sample is the replicate (ctrl 2 and treat 2 samples). With this few, only large and consistent changes are detectable, and the rank test cannot reach significance in small designs.
  • Each condition level is a different set of sections; anything else that differs between them (organ, tumour type, donor, processing) is part of the comparison (no BLOCK_COLUMN was given).
  • Visium pseudobulks mix the cell types of each domain: a DE gene can reflect a change in the cell-type mix within the label rather than in expression.
  • Log fold changes are not shrunken and the FDR is controlled within each label, not across labels; composition tests are CLR + t-test per label (scCODA is the fuller model).
